# GetFranchise 100 Ready-Candidate Plan

Date: 2026-10-04

## Decision

Franchisor outreach is paused until at least 100 brands pass the established
buyer-screening readiness validation. No emails, contact-form submissions, or
outreach status changes should be made before that threshold.

## Starting Point

- Portfolio: 484 brands
- Current ready candidates: 43
- Additional candidates required: 57
- Current bottlenecks: source depth, four verified core money fields, official
  sources, and evidence grade B- or better

"Ready" remains a buyer-screening standard, not an investment recommendation.
Direct franchisor confirmation, Canadian disclosure, entity review, unit
economics, closure/transfer evidence, and franchisee calls are still required
before a purchase decision.

## Research Sequence

1. Upgrade 60 brands in six batches of ten, prioritizing records that already
   have an official source or three or more sources.
2. For each brand, add traceable Canadian or brand-level sources and label any
   U.S. figures as context only.
3. Verify or explicitly mark unavailable: total investment, franchise fee,
   royalty, marketing fee, and liquid-capital requirement.
4. Run the readiness validation after every batch; retain only evidence-backed
   promotions and keep all other brands in the research queue.
5. When the count reaches 100, create a ranked outreach shortlist. Contact only
   that shortlist, not the entire 100-brand pool.

## Batch Priority

1. Beverage/dessert, limited-service food, and full-service food records that
   are close to the gate.
2. Home services, senior care, education, shipping/printing, staffing, and
   cleaning brands with existing official sources.
3. Fitness, pet services, automotive, finance, child services, and hotel
   records with credible Canadian availability.

## Acceptance Rule

Each promoted profile must pass all seven current checks:

- 5+ source links
- official source present
- 4/5 core money fields checked
- B- evidence grade or higher
- Canadian relevance stated
- cross-border figures labelled
- franchisor-confirmation boundary stated

The app must keep the outreach route locked until the ready count is at least
100. The 100-count gate authorizes planning and drafting only; a human must
still approve every individual contact before sending.

## Hermes Execution

Use `HERMES_100_CANDIDATE_COMPLETION_PROMPT.md` for the evidence-first agent
brief. It requires at least five distinct direct sources for every promoted or
newly discovered franchise candidate and prohibits all third-party outreach.
