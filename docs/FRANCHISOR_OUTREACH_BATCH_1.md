# Franchisor Outreach - Batch 1

> **PAUSED — 2026-10-04:** Do not contact franchisors from this list until the
> portfolio has at least 100 ready candidates. The current verified screening
> count is 43. This document is retained as a future outreach template and
> contact queue only.

Use this batch to ask franchisors to review their GetFranchise profile before the public launch.

The tone should be professional, neutral, and easy to respond to. We are not asking them to advertise. We are asking them to correct or confirm public information.

---

## Email To Send

Subject: Review request for your GetFranchise profile

Hi `[Franchise Development Team]`,

We are preparing GetFranchise, a Canadian franchise research directory for prospective franchise buyers.

We have created a preliminary profile for `[Brand Name]` using publicly available information. Before we publish or expand the profile, we would like to give your team the opportunity to review it and send any corrections.

We are especially looking to confirm:

- whether the brand is currently offering franchises in Canada
- current investment range in CAD
- franchise fee, royalty, marketing fee, and other recurring fees
- required liquid capital or net worth
- Canadian signing entity
- available territories
- the best official franchise page or contact person

If any information is outdated, incomplete, or should be marked as not publicly disclosed, please let us know. We will review any correction before updating the profile.

Best,

`[Your Name]`  
GetFranchise  
`corrections@getfranchise.ca`
